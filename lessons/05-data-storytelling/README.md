# Lesson 05: Data Storytelling

## What This Lesson Covers

How to turn data into a slide that tells someone what happened, why it matters, and what to do about it. The core skill is writing **Takeaway Titles** — slide titles that state the insight, not just the topic.

Based on Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic's *Storytelling with Data* principles.

## Scenario

A startup spent $100,000 across four marketing channels. Students analyze the results and build a slide that tells the CMO where to spend next quarter's budget.

## How the Class Works (90 minutes)

| Time | Minutes | What Happens |
|------|---------|--------------|
| 0:00 | 10 | Opening + DC ACT framework |
| 0:10 | 8 | Introduce the marketing channel dataset |
| 0:18 | 20 | **Group Exercise #1** — groups build their first slide (before instruction) |
| 0:38 | 12 | Gap Reveal + Knaflic principles for Takeaway Titles |
| 0:50 | 10 | **Group Exercise #2** — groups revise using the principles |
| 1:00 | 20 | 3 group presentations (7 min each) |
| 1:20 | 10 | Wrap-up + introduce take-home lesson exercises |

## Key Concepts

- **Takeaway Titles**: State the insight, not the category ("Email delivers 9x more conversions per dollar" vs. "Marketing Channel Performance")
- **Callouts**: Circle, arrow, or highlight that draws the eye to the evidence
- **Recommendation format**: [Action] → [Expected outcome]
- **DC ACT framework**: Define, Collect, Analyze, **Communicate**, Act

## Prerequisites

- Lessons 01–04 (students need context for the "Communicate" step of DC ACT)
- No SQL required for this lesson

## Lesson Exercises (Take-Home)

Students choose one of two "bad" slides (Website Traffic or Employee Satisfaction), find the insight, and build a single slide with a Takeaway Title, callout, and recommendation. Submit as a single-page PDF.
